## Description

```text
Emerald Isle is a lore-friendly RimWorld expansion inspired by Irish history, archaeology, material culture, language, and mythology.

[h1]Version 0.7.1 - Hearth, Larder, and Hospitality[/h1]

Version 0.7 makes the central hearth a more durable part of a developed colony.
The hearth cooks faster, burns less wood, casts more light, allows fuel ejection,
and supports the vanilla Flame meditation pattern while retaining its heat and
gathering role.

New Cooking 4 bills turn raw meat into a deliberately inefficient simple-tier
reserve that lasts 30 days unrefrigerated. After Beer brewing research, the hearth
can also combine raw oats and hops into vanilla wort for fermentation in vanilla
barrels. This adds no new alcohol, barrel, fermentation system, or research.

Version 0.7.1 replaces the older Workshop banner with the approved Hearth & Larder
image. Gameplay is unchanged from v0.7.0.

[h1]Current content[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Oats[/b] - A medium-cycle grain crop with hand-quern milling, oat porridge, oat flatbread, and exact-linear x4 production bills.
[*][b]Dry-stone wall[/b] - A material-efficient linked stone wall with original artwork.
[*][b]Flax and linen[/b] - A household textile path whose finished linen favors warm-weather clothing and can substitute in selected vanilla recipes.
[*][b]Linen tunic[/b] - A linen-only everyday garment with original ground and worn artwork.
[*][b]Brat cloak[/b] - A cheap shell-layer cloak with strong cold insulation in wool, adequate warmth in cloth, and much less warmth in linen.
[*][b]Central hearth[/b] - A permanent stone household hearth providing heat, light, gathering, Flame meditation, fuel control, and slower cooking without electricity.
[*][b]Smoked meat[/b] - A hearth-only, Cooking 4 reserve food with a 30-day unrefrigerated shelf life and fresh-meal-friendly selection priority.
[*][b]Oat wort[/b] - A researched hearth recipe using 20 raw oats and 5 hops to make 5 vanilla wort for vanilla barrel fermentation.
[*][b]Kerry cattle[/b] - Small, hardy dairy cattle with lower feed use and milk output per animal than vanilla cows.
[*][b]Farmhouse cheese[/b] - A 35-day milk preserve made at the central hearth or vanilla fueled and electric stoves.
[*][b]Wolfhound[/b] - A fast, rough-coated domestic fighter with Intermediate Guard and Attack training, limited utility, high food cost, and uncommon trader availability.
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]

[b]Requires RimWorld 1.6 Core.[/b] No DLC is required. Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly, and Odyssey are supported. Odyssey adds Attack Target training to the wolfhound through the vanilla combat-canine pattern.

Existing saves can add Version 0.7.1 without custom migration. Back up important saves before changing any mod list.

[url=https://github.com/PatrickMee/emerald-isle/blob/main/docs/release/v0.7.1.md]Version 0.7.1 release notes[/url]
```

# v0.7.0: Hearth, Larder, and Hospitality

**Status:** Release candidate; maintainer game testing passed<br>
**Required:** RimWorld 1.6 Core<br>
**Officially supported DLC:** Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly, Odyssey<br>
**Required DLC:** None

**Artifact:** `EmeraldIsle-v0.7.0.zip`<br>
**Steam Workshop:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763433723

## Summary

Version 0.7.0 makes the central hearth a more durable part of a developed colony.
It improves the hearth's everyday utility, adds smoked meat as a deliberately
inefficient 30-day reserve food, and lets oat-growing colonies make vanilla wort
from oats and hops without introducing a separate brewing system.

## Included Content

### Central Hearth

- Work speed increases from `0.45` to `0.65` of a full-speed worktable.
- Fuel use decreases from `8` to `6` wood per day; its existing capacity now lasts
  about 5.3 days when full.
- Glow radius increases from `9` to `10`, and stored fuel can be ejected.
- Existing heat, gathering-spot, recipes, art, DefName, and save contract remain.
- Flame meditation uses the verified vanilla campfire behavior and remains
  compatible when Royalty is absent.

### Smoked Meat

- New hearth-only `smoke meat` and `smoke meat x4` bills require Cooking 4.
- One smoked meat consumes `0.60` nutrition of raw meat, takes `600` work, and
  provides `0.80` nutrition with a 30-day unrefrigerated shelf life.
- It is a simple-tier food with reserve-food selection priority, so fresh meals
  remain the normal choice.
- Human, insect, and Anomaly twisted meat are disallowed by default, while normal
  ingredient provenance, thoughts, and food-poisoning behavior are preserved.
- The original smoked-meat icon is included with recorded provenance.

### Oat Wort

- After Beer brewing research, the central hearth can turn `20` raw oats and `5`
  hops into `5` vanilla wort.
- The wort uses vanilla fermenting barrels and becomes vanilla beer; this release
  adds no new alcohol, barrel, fermentation system, research, or drug effect.
- Hearth production is slower than making wort at the vanilla brewery.

### Usability Corrections

- Central-hearth recipes are registered exactly once, removing the duplicate
  smoked-meat and farmhouse-cheese entries found during testing.
- The brat cloak description now explains that wool provides strong cold-weather
  insulation, cloth is adequate, and linen sacrifices much of its warmth. Its
  stats and recipes are unchanged.
- The v0.6.4 oat-flatbread reserve-food priority correction remains included.

## Installation and Upgrade

Subscribe on the Steam Workshop for automatic installation, or manually replace
the existing `EmeraldIsle` folder with the exact release package. Do not merge
folders or install multiple Emerald Isle versions side by side.

Existing saves and central hearths remain compatible because released DefNames and
serialized contracts are preserved. Back up important saves before changing any
mod list.

## Known Issues

No release-blocking issue is known.

## Rollback

The prior stable release is `v0.6.4`, **Oat Flatbread Food Priority**. Preserve its
exact GitHub artifact and Workshop content as the rollback target. Before loading a
v0.7.0 save on v0.6.4, remove smoked meat, oat-wort bills, and other references to
new v0.7.0 definitions; otherwise RimWorld may report missing definitions.
